Script Follow-Along does not collect, transmit, or share any personal
information.

## Data stored on your device

Your projects — show names, imported PDF references, cue index, and settings —
are saved in a local file on your device. This data never leaves your device and
is never accessible to us or any third party.

## Network use

The app listens for OSC (Open Sound Control) messages on your local network to
provide live cue navigation. It does not send any data over the network and does
not communicate with any external servers.

## Analytics and tracking

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
